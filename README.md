**From call audio to next steps.** Turns recorded or uploaded meeting and sales calls into a speaker-labeled transcript, summary, sentiment, action items, risks, decisions, and a draft follow-up email.

### 🦠 OutbreakAI
**Decision-support prototype.** Records disease activity by region, assigns a risk level, tracks case trends, and suggests responses like preparing hospital overflow capacity or running vaccination drives.

> ⚠️ The live risk score is rule-based. The included ML artifacts are not used in the API's risk decisions, and it is not a validated outbreak-prediction system.

`Python` `Flask` `SQLAlchemy` `SQLite / PostgreSQL` `OpenWeatherMap`

[Repo](https://github.com/Eng-Hu-snain-Ali/OutbreakAI) · [Known limitations](https://github.com/Eng-Hu-snain-Ali/OutbreakAI/blob/main/README.md#known-limitations)

</td>

### 🚌 Bus-Seat-Reservation-System
**Front-end booking prototype.** Search bus trips, compare sample schedules, choose seats, and see a booking confirmation.

> ⚠️ Schedules and seats are hard-coded and the flow is simulated in the browser. Seats stay booked only for the current page session. No backend or database.

`HTML` `CSS` `Vanilla JavaScript`

[Repo](https://github.com/Eng-Hu-snain-Ali/Bus-Seat-Reservation-System) · [Booking code](https://github.com/Eng-Hu-snain-Ali/Bus-Seat-Reservation-System/blob/main/Bus%20go/script.js)
